Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept insurance?

Yes — Aetna, BlueCross/BlueShield, Cigna/Evernorth, Healthcare Highways, and UnitedHealthcare (UHC/UBH) are accepted. Private-pay sessions are $150, and a free 15-minute consultation is available to see if it's a good fit.

What can I expect in a first session?

Your first session is a chance to share what brought you to therapy and ask any questions. Expect a conversation about your history and goals, and a plan for how we'll work together going forward.

Do you offer virtual sessions?

Yes — sessions are available in-person at the Georgetown, TX office or online via video, whichever fits your schedule best.
